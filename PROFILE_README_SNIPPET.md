### Featured research — Hakaluki Haor (Bangladesh)

**Wetland inundation, atmospheric methane and hydroclimatic controls | 2019–2025**

An independent, reproducible remote-sensing research workflow integrating Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2, Dynamic World, TROPOMI XCH4, CHIRPS, ERA5-Land, Google Earth Engine and Python. Current outputs include 84 monthly records, initial Earth Engine maps, exploratory statistical analyses, and a documented validation roadmap. Investigation of ESA Biomass P-band, atmospheric transport/inversion and field-flux calibration is planned, not yet completed.
